## Users

Confirmed: prospective freelance buyers assessing Ivan Delgado Galvan's independent frontend and design work.
Authored demo audience: people planning a short nature escape on mobile or desktop.

## Product Purpose

Create a third independent website for the user's portfolio, with design driven primarily by pbakaus/impeccable and supported by OpenDesign resources. The user delegated the subject: "otra web de lo que se te ocurra".

## Operating Context

A personal, fictional travel brand with local sample data. On 2026-11-16, after reviewing the demo, the user explicitly authorized publication in a public repository on their personal GitHub account, ivaaandeelgado. The user requested extra space above the hero and richer header hover states, and approved the rest of the visual design. This authorization covers the public source repository and downloadable demo; no hosting deployment or real booking service is requested.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Authored scope: browse six fictional stays, filter by landscape/party size/budget, save favorites, inspect a stay, choose check-in/check-out, see a validated itemized estimate, and save/export a local itinerary. Invalid dates, capacity limits, unavailable sample dates, empty results and storage failures have explicit recovery paths.
No real booking, payment, email delivery, authentication or live availability. No API keys, trackers, analytics or paid services. No employer resources. No invented client endorsements or delivery claims.

## Brand Commitments

Senda is a fictional brand authored for this project. Spanish primary copy. Personal author: Ivan Delgado Galvan. Do not expose personal contact details in the demo.

## Evidence on Hand

Two earlier WordPress portfolio websites, Luma and Miga, and an Angular demo exist independently. They establish commercial purpose but do not dictate this new visual identity.
All stays, prices, locations within the fictional catalogue and availability are illustrative. Photography must carry source and license provenance and must not be represented as a photograph of an actual bookable property.

## Product Principles

- Make the next planning step obvious.
- Show the price breakdown before saving a plan.
- Keep fictional demonstrations distinct from real commercial commitments.
- Work with keyboard, touch and reduced motion.
- Retain the user's selections only on their own device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Implementation target: WCAG 2.2 AA practices, keyboard-operable dialogs, explicit labels, visible focus, reduced-motion support, touch-sized controls and no horizontal scrolling at 320px.
